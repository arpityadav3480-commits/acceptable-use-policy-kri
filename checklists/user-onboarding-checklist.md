# New User Security Onboarding Checklist

**Based on:** KRI-ISP-001 Acceptable Use Policy v1.0 · Kestrelwood Research Institute (*fictional*)
**Completed by:** the new user, with their line manager or PI, during the first week

| # | Item | Policy section | Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read the Acceptable Use Policy and sign the acknowledgement form | 15 | ☐ |
| 2 | Set a passphrase of at least 14 characters and save it in the KRI password manager | 7.2 | ☐ |
| 3 | Enrol in MFA with an authenticator app or security key | 7.3 | ☐ |
| 4 | Confirm that the KRI laptop has encryption, endpoint protection and automatic updates enabled (IT to verify) | 8.1 | ☐ |
| 5 | If using a personal phone for KRI email: screen lock on, OS supported, KRI app protection installed | 8.2 | ☐ |
| 6 | Know how to lock your screen (Windows + L) | 5.9 | ☐ |
| 7 | Know where to store work files (approved KRI storage) and which services are on the Approved Services List | 5.4, 9.4 | ☐ |
| 8 | Understand the four classification levels and the handling rules for your project's data | 9.1 | ☐ |
| 9 | If handling research participant data: read the project's ethics approval and data management plan | 9.2 | ☐ |
| 10 | Know which AI tools are approved, and that Confidential and Restricted data must never go into public AI tools | 5.5 | ☐ |
| 11 | Save the incident contacts (IT Helpdesk extension 4400 · security@kestrelwood.example), and know that a lost device or suspected account compromise must be reported within 1 hour | 8.3, 10.2 | ☐ |
| 12 | Find the **Report Phishing** button in your email client | 10.2 | ☐ |
| 13 | Complete the security awareness training module | 4 | ☐ |
